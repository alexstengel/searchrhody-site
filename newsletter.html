What’s inside each issue

The Rhody Roundup is SearchRhody’s email newsletter. When we start sending, the plan is a weekly letter you can read in a few minutes and use the same week. We’ll confirm that cadence when the first issue is ready. We don’t claim a subscriber count, and there isn’t a public archive yet.

A place to explore

A shoreline, a walk, or a town worth a Saturday — shared after we’ve been there ourselves.

Something made in Rhode Island

A maker or independent shop we’re getting to know, never named until we have permission.

A weekend idea

One simple plan for time together, without a packed itinerary or a hard sell.
