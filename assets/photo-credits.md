# SearchRhody photo credits

Licensed Rhode Island landscape and landmark photographs used on the SearchRhody 2.0 preview site. Every image was cropped and resized for the web (WebP and JPEG). None are AI-generated, and none are used to represent specific people as “locals.”

Source files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and are kept locally only as references; they are not part of the public site files.

- Subject: View from Beavertail Lighthouse, Beavertail State Park, Jamestown, Rhode Island
- Photographer: Leonardo DaSilva
- License: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_from_the_top_of_the_Light_House_-_Beavertail_State_Park_-_Jamestown_RI_-_panoramio_(2).jpg)
