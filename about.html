About

An independent guide to the Ocean State

SearchRhody is a publication-led discovery guide. We look for interesting places, local makers, unique products, and family-friendly experiences — and we share what we find with care.

Our mission

There’s more to Rhode Island than most people see in a weekend. We want to make the good stuff easier to find: the shop you’d miss if you only drove the main road, the maker selling something honestly made here, the outing that turns a Saturday into a story.

The founders

We didn’t grow up in Rhode Island. We moved here, made it home, and kept finding new places we wished we’d discovered sooner. SearchRhody is our way of sharing those finds—and meeting more of the people who make this little state special.

Editorial independence

SearchRhody is an independent publication. We are not a newsroom with a reporting desk, and we will not invent reviews, ratings, or sponsorships. If a story is sponsored or includes an approved affiliate relationship, we will say so clearly on the page. See our disclosures for the current policy.
