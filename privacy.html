Privacy

Privacy

This page explains how the SearchRhody website handles information. SearchRhody is an independent Rhode Island discovery guide and newsletter, not an account or checkout system.

What this site collects

This site does not set its own analytics cookies, and does not ask you to create an account or enter a payment card.

If you subscribe to The Rhody Roundup, we collect your email address, an optional first name, and your consent to receive the newsletter. We use Buttondown to store subscriber emails and send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time through the link in every email. We will not sell your information.

If you contact us by email, we will use your address and the contents of your message to reply. Contact inquiries are not added to a newsletter or marketing list.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google may receive your IP address and standard request data under Google’s own policies.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, use the Contact page. The editorial email address will be added there before launch.
