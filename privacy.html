Privacy

SearchRhody is a local visibility service based in Johnston, Rhode Island. This page explains how this website handles information. It is a simple marketing site, not an account or checkout system.

What this site collects

This site does not use a contact form, does not set its own analytics cookies, and does not ask you to create an account or enter a payment card.

If you email getfound@searchrhody.com, we will use your address and the contents of your message to reply and, if we work together, to provide the service we discuss. We do not sell your information.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google may receive your IP address and standard request data under Google's own policies.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, email getfound@searchrhody.com.
