Say hello

We welcome editorial tips, partnership inquiries, and corrections. This is not a service intake form, and messages sent here will not be added to a marketing or newsletter list.

Editorial tips

Know a place, maker, or outing we should look at? Tell us what it is and why it matters. We’ll follow up if we can take a closer look. We don’t publish a tip until we’ve done our own reporting and, when a person is featured, received permission.

Partnerships

If you’re interested in a partnership, please write with who you are and what you have in mind. We don’t currently have approved partners or paid placements, and we won’t add undisclosed affiliate links.

Corrections

If something we published is wrong, we want to know. Send the page, the issue, and the correct information if you have it.

Email

The editorial inbox is an open decision and is not published yet. When an address is chosen, it will appear here as a mailto link. Please don’t use older service addresses as a newsletter signup — contact mail will be used to reply, not to start a subscription.
