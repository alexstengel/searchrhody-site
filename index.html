FAQ

A few straightforward answers

What does "showing up in AI answers" mean?

When someone asks a tool like ChatGPT "who's a good roofer near me?", some businesses get mentioned and others don't. Helping your business be findable in those answers is sometimes called generative engine optimization, or GEO. In plain terms: we make sure your public info is clear, consistent, and complete, and we set up the profiles those tools can actually use. There's no switch that guarantees you'll be named.

Is this a guarantee I'll rank first or get recommended?

No. Nobody honest can promise that. Listings, reviews, and consistent information help people and search tools understand your business. They don't control the results, and I won't pretend they do.

Do you work for Google or OpenAI?

No. I'm independent. I'll use their public tools and profile pages on your behalf. I'm not affiliated with Google or OpenAI.

Who is this for?

Small service businesses in Rhode Island — plumbers, landscapers, HVAC, salons, and similar owner-run shops — especially if you have little or no online presence yet. If you're outside that, email me anyway and I'll tell you whether I can help.

Do I need a website?

A website helps, but the core package is about your listings and your information around the web. If you don't have a site, we can still get a lot done, and we can talk about what's useful later.

What do you need from me?

Access to your Google Business Profile (or help creating one), a clear list of your hours and services, and a little time for questions. I'll tell you exactly what I need before we start.

Do you replace a marketing agency?

No. SearchRhody is the first step, not a substitute for an agency. We set up the essentials — a claimed, accurate profile, photos, review replies, and a simple way to catch inquiries — so you have a presence. When you're ready for more, you get a clean handoff kit for whoever comes next. There is no long contract to get started.

When will the prices be final?

The names and numbers marked in yellow, like [Package price], are placeholders. Final offer and pricing copy is still being written. Email me and I'll share the current details.
