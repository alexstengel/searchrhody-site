FAQ

A few straightforward answers

Do I have to sign up for monthly service?

No. Get Found is a standalone, one-time service. Monthly support is completely optional.

Can I cancel monthly support?

Yes. Stay Found is month to month, with no annual commitment. Specific billing and cancellation terms will be provided before enrollment.

Do I own my Google Business Profile?

Yes. You retain ownership and control. SearchRhody works through authorized access, and your profile stays yours.

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google Business Profile itself is free. You're paying SearchRhody for the time and work involved in reviewing, preparing, and optimizing your business presence.

Will I rank first on Google or be recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee that. We focus on accurate information, complete profiles, and practical improvements that make your business easier for customers and search tools to understand.

Does AI do all the work?

We use AI to assist with research, content preparation, and reporting. All customer information and public-facing changes are reviewed before implementation.

What about AI search optimization?

We can review publicly accessible information for clarity and consistency, but we do not guarantee AI recommendations or offer an unverified ChatGPT profile setup.

Do you work for Google or OpenAI?

No. SearchRhody is independent and not affiliated with Google or OpenAI. We use their public tools and profile pages on your behalf.

Do I need a website?

A website helps, but this work is about your Google Business Profile and publicly accessible business information. If you don't have a site, we can still get a lot done. Website design and development are not included.

What do you need from me?

Accurate business details, photos, and access to your Google Business Profile (or help creating one if a straightforward setup is possible). We'll tell you exactly what we need before we start.
