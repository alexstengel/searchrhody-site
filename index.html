FAQ

A few straightforward answers

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. The setup is a one-time project, and monthly support is month to month. You can stop anytime.

Can I just do the setup and skip the monthly plan?

Yes. Plenty of businesses only need the setup. You'll get a handoff kit either way.

Who owns my profiles?

You do. Your profiles stay in your name and under your control, and the handoff kit puts everything in one place.

What does "showing up in AI answers" mean?

When someone asks a tool like ChatGPT "who's a good roofer near me?", some businesses get mentioned and others don't. We make sure your public info is clear, consistent, and complete, and set up the profiles those tools can use. There's no switch that guarantees you'll be named.

Will this guarantee I rank first or get more customers?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. The fixes show up right away. Search visibility can improve over time, but no one controls the results.

Do you work for Google or OpenAI?

No. I'm independent and not affiliated with Google or OpenAI. I use their public tools and profile pages on your behalf.

Do I need a website?

A website helps, but the setup is about your listings and your info around the web. If you don't have a site, we can still get a lot done.

What do you need from me?

Access to your Google Business Profile (or help creating one), your hours and services, and a little time for questions. I'll tell you exactly what I need before we start.

When will prices be final?

The names and numbers in brackets, like [Package price], are placeholders while final pricing is set. Email me and I'll share the current details.
