A few things worth knowing

Services cover one business location

Get Found and Stay Found each cover one business location. Stay Found is available as a continuation of Get Found.

What you provide

Customers provide accurate business details, photos, and authorized account access. Get Found works with an existing eligible Google Business Profile, or a straightforward new setup where verification is possible. You approve public-facing content before it goes live.

What's not included

Complicated verification, suspended profile recovery, extensive directory changes, website development, and paid advertising are not included.

No guaranteed rankings, leads, or AI recommendations

Search rankings, leads, approval timelines, and AI recommendations are not guaranteed. We focus on accurate information, complete profiles, and practical, approved improvements.

Monthly support has defined activity limits

Stay Found includes one monthly profile health check, approved business information updates, up to two profile posts or photo updates, and up to five drafted review responses. There is no long-term service commitment. Billing and cancellation terms will be provided before you enroll.

FAQ

A few straightforward answers

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Get Found is a one-time service, and Stay Found is optional monthly support. There's no annual commitment or required ongoing plan.

Can I cancel Stay Found whenever I want?

Yes. Stay Found is month to month. You can request cancellation without an annual commitment or early termination penalty. We'll explain the billing and cancellation details before you enroll.

Do I have to sign up for monthly service?

No. Get Found is a standalone, one-time service. Monthly support is completely optional.

Do I own my Google Business Profile?

Your Google Business Profile stays yours. SearchRhody works through authorized access, and you retain ownership and control of your business information.

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google Business Profile itself is free. You're paying SearchRhody for the time and work involved in reviewing, preparing, and optimizing your business presence.

Will I rank first on Google or be recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee that. We focus on accurate information, complete profiles, and practical improvements that make your business easier for customers and search tools to understand.

Does AI do all the work?

We use AI to assist with research, content preparation, and reporting. All customer information and public-facing changes are reviewed before implementation.

What about AI search optimization?

We can review publicly accessible information for clarity and consistency, but we do not guarantee AI recommendations or offer an unverified ChatGPT profile setup.

Do you work for Google or OpenAI?

No. SearchRhody is independent and not affiliated with Google or OpenAI. We use their public tools and profile pages on your behalf.

Do I need a website?

A website helps, but this work is about your Google Business Profile and publicly accessible business information. If you don't have a site, we can still get a lot done. Website design and development are not included.

What do you need from me?

Accurate business details, photos, and access to your Google Business Profile (or help creating one if a straightforward setup is possible). We'll tell you exactly what we need before we start.
